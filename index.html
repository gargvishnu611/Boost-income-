06 / ABOUT AARVIX

Utility before a token.

The platform should be valuable because it helps people and teams get useful work done — not because someone expects a token price to rise.

ARVX is a planned future crypto asset, not a launched token. No allocation, exchange listing, redemption rate or conversion is promised. Any future launch would require a working product, legal review, technical security and transparent disclosures.

07 / FAQ

Good questions deserve clear answers.

Can I earn real money today?

No. This is an early demo. It does not contain funded client tasks or real payouts.

Are demo XP or credits cash?

No. They are illustrative progress values and have no cash value or guaranteed conversion.

How will paid work be funded?

A client or sponsor must fund a campaign before the task is shown as a paid opportunity. Accepted work is paid under the published terms.

Is ARVX available?

No. ARVX has not launched. This website has no token, wallet, deposits, withdrawals or exchange features.

Can referrals generate money?

A referral reward may be offered only when a real campaign budget funds it and an invited member completes a meaningful verified activity. Recruitment alone is not paid.

Is the member dashboard a real account?

No. The dashboard preview stores demo activity in this browser only. It is not synced or protected by production authentication.
